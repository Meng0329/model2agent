# Rice GRWT100 selection (B1)

- Real Ridge model (alpha=1000, 2000 pruned SNPs) ranked all 904 candidates (E1).
- 20 real phenotyping queries (E3) on the model top-20; measured 2.8-5.0 g.
- Final top-10 selected by measured GRWT100 (ties by E1 prediction); all >=3.4 g.
- Limitations: E1 != E3; ~0.45 Spearman; single env; linear additive; only 20 lines phenotyped; quartile threshold inferred, not observed.
